FAQ

Common Questions

What budget do I need to start with Google Ads?

We recommend a minimum of $500–$1,000/month in ad spend for meaningful results in most local markets. Our management fee is separate. A smaller budget can work in less competitive areas — we'll advise you on what's realistic in your market on our first call.

Can I pause or stop the ads any time?

Yes. You're never locked in. You control the budget and can pause or stop your campaigns at any time. We don't believe in contracts that trap clients — we earn your business every month by delivering results.

Do you write the ads or do I?

We handle everything — keyword research, ad copy, ad extensions, and landing page recommendations. You don't need to write a single word. We'll ask for your input on tone and key selling points, but the execution is 100% on us.

Why should I use an agency instead of running ads myself?

Unmanaged campaigns waste 40–60% of budget on irrelevant clicks. We eliminate that waste from day one through proper campaign structure, negative keywords, and ongoing optimisation. The cost of management typically pays for itself within the first month through reduced wasted spend alone.
