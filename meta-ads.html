FAQ

Common Questions

What's the difference between Facebook Ads and Google Ads?

Google captures people already searching for you — high intent, ready to buy. Meta creates demand by showing your business to people who don't know you yet but fit your exact customer profile. They work best together, but Meta is especially powerful for building local brand awareness and reaching new audiences at scale.

Do I need a Facebook Business page?

Yes. All Meta ads run through a Facebook Business page. If you don't have one, we'll help you set it up and optimise it as part of the onboarding process — no extra charge.

Is creative (images/videos) included in the service?

Yes. We handle all ad creative design as part of the package. This includes image ads, carousel ads, and basic video and motion graphics where applicable. You don't need to supply creative — we build it for you.

How much should I spend on Meta Ads?

We recommend a minimum of $300–$500/month in ad spend to generate consistent results in a local market. Results scale with budget — higher spend means more data, faster optimisation, and more leads. Our management fee is separate and covers everything from creative to reporting.
