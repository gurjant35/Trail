FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a website take to build?

Typically 2–4 weeks from discovery call to launch, depending on complexity. Simple sites often launch faster. We'll give you a clear timeline on our first call.

Do I own the website after it's done?

Yes. 100%. You own all the files, domain, and hosting. We hand everything over at launch. There are no lock-in contracts or proprietary platforms that trap your content.

Will my website show up on Google?

Yes. Every site we build is SEO-optimised and submitted to Google Search Console on launch day. We also provide proper on-page SEO structure, schema markup, and meta tags from the start.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. We do full redesigns as well as new builds. If your current site isn't converting or ranking, a redesign can make an immediate and measurable difference.
