FAQ

Common Questions

How long until I rank higher on Google Maps?

Most clients see ranking improvements within 30–60 days. Significant results — including consistent top-3 positioning — typically appear within 90 days of full optimisation and active management.

Do I need a physical address to use GBP?

No. Service-area businesses — those that travel to customers rather than hosting them at a fixed location — can still rank locally without displaying a public address. We set this up correctly from the start.

What happens if a competitor reports my listing?

We monitor your profile around the clock and handle any issues, disputes, or reinstatement requests on your behalf. Your listing stays protected without you having to lift a finger.

Is this a one-time service or ongoing?

It's ongoing. Google rewards consistent activity — regular posts, fresh reviews, updated photos, and profile maintenance. One-time optimisations fade. Our monthly management keeps you at the top.
